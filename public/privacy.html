Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Pentagonal operates the OAuth2 forwarding service at transistor.pentagonal.org (the "Service"). The Service lets a WordPress site connect a Gmail account through Google sign-in without every site registering its own Google Cloud OAuth client. This policy explains what the Service does with your data. In short: it processes almost nothing, and stores nothing.

What the Service does

When you click "Login with Gmail" in your WordPress admin, Google sends your browser back to the Service with an authorization code. The Service checks the request, then forwards your browser to your own WordPress site. Your site then exchanges the code for tokens directly with Google. The Service never receives, stores, or handles those tokens.

Data processed in transit

For each authorization request, the Service processes the following in memory, only for the time needed to handle that single request:

  • The authorization code from Google — passed through to your site, never stored, never logged.
  • The state parameter — a small JSON object your site created (a random nonce, an expiry time, and your site's callback URL). The Service validates it and forwards it back unchanged. It is never stored.
  • Technical data needed to deliver the request over the internet (such as your IP address and browser information), processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Data we store

None. The Service has no database, no user accounts, no sessions, and no cookies. It does not run analytics and does not track you across sites. Once a request is answered, everything about it is discarded.

What we never touch

  • Your emails — the Service never reads, sends, or stores mail. The Gmail scope requested is gmail.send only, and it is your WordPress site (not the Service) that sends mail using the tokens it holds.
  • Your Google account data — handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.
  • Your access or refresh tokens — stored on your own WordPress site only, never on the Service. How your site stores them is your site administrator's responsibility.

Data retention and deletion

Because the Service stores no personal data, there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. If you want the tokens held by your WordPress site removed, disconnect the Gmail account from your site's admin panel and revoke the app's access in your Google account permissions.

Children

The Service is a tool for website administrators. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
