Pentagonal OAuth Relay

The Pentagonal OAuth Relay is a free, public forwarding service for OAuth2 sign-in. It exists for one reason: letting a WordPress site offer one-click Google sign-in ("Login with Gmail") without every site having to register its own Google Cloud OAuth client.

How it works

Your WordPress site (for example, one running the TrayDigita Mailer plugin) sends you to Google to sign in and allow the app. Google then sends you back — not directly to your site, but to this relay. The relay checks the request and forwards your browser to your own site, which finishes the sign-in directly with Google.

The relay is deliberately boring: it holds no tokens, no accounts, no sessions, and no database. It cannot read your mail, and it stores nothing about you. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Supported providers

  • Google — supported. Sign in with a Google account to connect Gmail (send-only scope).
  • Microsoft, Yahoo — planned, not available yet.

Who runs it

The service is operated by Pentagonal and runs on Cloudflare Workers. The code and the specification are public: github.com/ArrayIterator/oauth2-forwarders.
